🌌🖖 Greetings, fellow xenophile!

Sagittarius Species is the portrait pack for anyone who's ever wanted their Stellaris empire to look like it walked straight out of a con hall, a tabletop splatbook, and a late-night sci-fi marathon all at once. AI-assisted art, a shared animated rig, and a genuine excuse to roleplay as a race of sapient furries who also happen to run the galaxy — what's not to love?

We're currently rolling **18 unique species** into your character creator:

[b]Humans (augmented realism):[/b] The baseline build, min-maxed with a few cybernetic upgrades.
[b]Space Elves:[/b] Pointy ears, longer lifespans, and a save-vs-arrogance they usually fail.
[b]High Elves:[/b] Space Elves, but they read the lore books and never let you forget it.
[b]Green Elves:[/b] Half elf, half houseplant, fully judging your terraforming choices.
[b]Atlantis Space:[/b] Deep-sea royalty who traded the ocean for the void and kept the tridents.
[b]Astral Humans:[/b] Rolled a natural 20 on Cosmic Awareness; now they just kind of glow.
[b]Necromancers:[/b] Necromancy but make it space opera — dark rituals, darker mana bills.
[b]Furries:[/b] Fur, tails, and the confidence of a species that knows it's the fan-favorite.
[b]Mollusk:[/b] Tentacled, unbothered, definitely plotting something from the deep.
[b]Lovecraftian Mollusk:[/b] Their cousin who took one too many trips beyond the veil.
[b]Order of the Green Knights:[/b] Nature-worshipping paladins with excellent tree-hugging PR.
[b]Shaw's Birds:[/b] Avian aces who treat orbital combat like a really aggressive game of chicken.
[b]New Vargrosians:[/b] Heirs to a culture ancient enough to have opinions about your architecture.
[b]Order of Red Soldiers:[/b] Machine-empire zealots running on unwavering loyalty and questionable firmware.
[b]Cyborgs:[/b] Flesh-and-chrome overachievers who upgraded before it was cool.
[b]Timbot:[/b] Small, blocky, and somehow the most beloved unit in your entire fleet.
[b]Mercenaries:[/b] Guns, contracts, and zero interest in your empire's backstory.
[b]Star Knight:[/b] Sworn protectors of the void, lightsaber-adjacent energy strongly implied.

New in this version: most of these species just got a rig overhaul — a corrected UV layout on the shared animated portrait means bigger, better-centered art instead of half the canvas going to waste. Fewer wasted pixels, more face to admire while your pop grows old and dies for the Empire.

Bugs, suggestions, unhinged lore requests — the comment section is open and we read all of it. Come help shape this mod into something even more delightfully extra. Live long, and terraform responsibly. 🖖✨
